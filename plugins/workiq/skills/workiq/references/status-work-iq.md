# Summarizing status

Apply to caller-owned summaries of retrieved evidence or structured reads and to
shortened delegated `ask` answers. This does not change tool routing or authorize
extra calls.

Before shortening, identify unresolved scope boundaries, dependencies, blockers,
owners and approval conditions that materially change the current status or next
decision. Preserve each material qualifier with its dated source citation;
compress background and repeated history instead. Keep distinct workstreams and
classification boundaries separate. A completed milestone does not establish
completion of later stages.

If evidence does not establish completion or approval, say it is unconfirmed,
not definitely complete or still pending. Use the substantive content date,
not merely a newer forwarding or modification timestamp. Do not revive
historical actions as current without evidence, or make extra calls solely to
repeat supported facts. Retain delegated-answer attribution; these safeguards
do not establish independent source verification.
